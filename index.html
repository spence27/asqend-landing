Before we start, we agree on the first system, the growth target, how we’ll measure it, and the start date. If we miss the agreed target by day 30, we refund the first 30 days of Asqend service fees, and you can end the engagement with no further Asqend service fees due. The exact refund amount is set out alongside the full engagement fee before you commit.

What does it cost?

We agree a fixed fee and scope for the 90-day engagement. That covers the strategy, agreed builds, rollout, training, and weekly reviews. You’ll hear a range on the first call and see the full cost, including any separate software costs, before you commit.

Do agents act on their own?

Only within rules we agree with you. Research, monitoring, and drafts can run in the background. Routine actions you’ve approved can run automatically. We define which actions need a person’s approval and what gets recorded before the system goes live.

We’re already stretched. How much work is this for us?

We own the implementation. You provide one accountable lead, a focused kickoff, access to the relevant tools and data, and people who can answer questions about the work. After that, we need timely feedback and approvals plus a short weekly review. We prepare the context and recommendations, handle the build and training, and agree your team’s responsibilities before we start.

What happens after 90 days?

You decide, with the scoreboard in front of you. You can expand to the next priorities, hand the systems to your team, or keep us on to run and improve them.
