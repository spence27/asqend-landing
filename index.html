A few practical questions

Before we talk.

What happens after the look-back?

You can continue with Asqend to keep the context current and work with me on what it surfaces. Custom builds are quoted separately. We discuss the likely ongoing cost before you pay for the look-back. Scope and pricing are agreed before anything starts; there is no automatic subscription or retainer.

What if we already use ChatGPT or Claude?

Keep using them. Asqend adds the business context. You can also connect through MCP to access Asqend information in ChatGPT, Claude or another compatible AI assistant.

Is this consulting or a SaaS product?

Both. Asqend is the core product: it gives you an ongoing understanding of what’s happening across your business. We can then work with you as consultants to build solutions for the challenges it uncovers.

Any limitations on the money-back guarantee?

No. If you don’t get a useful insight, you get your $1,000 back. You decide whether the readout was useful.

The last 30 days often reveal how much is happening outside your view, and how much there is to improve.

What do you need from us?

An agreed set of recent meeting history, a decision-maker who can act on useful findings, and time for the walkthrough. We confirm the connection or export path and the readout date on the first call, before payment.
