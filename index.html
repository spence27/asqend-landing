Questions CEOs ask

Straight answers.

How does the 30-day guarantee work?

Before we start, we agree on the first system, the growth target, how we’ll measure it, and the start date. If we miss the agreed target by day 30, we refund the first 30 days of Asqend service fees. You can end the engagement with no further Asqend service fees due. The exact refund amount is set out alongside the full engagement fee before you commit.

What does it cost?

We agree a fixed fee and scope for the 90-day engagement. That covers the strategy, agreed builds, rollout, training, and weekly reviews. You’ll hear a range on the first call and see the full cost, including any separate software costs, before you commit.

Do agents act on their own?

Only within rules we agree with you. Research, monitoring, and drafts can run in the background. Routine actions you’ve approved can run automatically. We define which actions need a person’s approval and what gets recorded before the system goes live.

What do you need from us?

A sponsor who can make decisions, access to the agreed systems, and a few people who know the work. We’ll need their input during setup, timely feedback and approvals, and a short weekly review. We agree those responsibilities and the start date before work begins.

What happens after 90 days?

You decide, with the scoreboard in front of you. You can expand to the next priorities, hand the systems to your team, or keep us on to run and improve them.
